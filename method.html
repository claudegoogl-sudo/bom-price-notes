How prices are compared

1. Write down the requirement

Each BOM line gets a short specification: electrical values, size, connector type, quantity and a maximum price.

2. Collect offers

I look at several shops (for example AliExpress, Amazon.de, LCSC and local German shops) and keep the best one to three offers per item.

3. Convert to the landed price

Total price = item price + shipping to Germany, in EUR. Orders above about 150 EUR can trigger import fees, so they are flagged.

4. Check quality signals

  • Seller rating of at least 95 percent.
  • At least 50 orders for the listing.
  • The listing text matches the specification (no copied photos with different parts).

5. Check the link

Before a link is published I open it again and confirm the page loads, the product is available and the price is parsed correctly.
