Finding the lowest landed price for electronics and maker parts

BOM Price Notes is a small personal project. I build hardware (3D printers, battery packs, small electronics) and every build starts with a bill of materials (BOM): a list of 5 to 30 parts such as BMS boards, buck-boost converters, connectors and nickel strip. Buying them from several shops is slow, so I compare offers and write down what I find.

This site publishes those notes: which part matched the specification, what it costs including shipping to Germany, and what to watch out for (wrong variant, too few orders, fake specifications).

What you will find here

  • Price comparisons per BOM item in EUR, delivered to Germany.
  • Checks on seller rating, order count and the real specification of the part.
  • Short notes on parts that look right but are not (for example nickel strip that is not pure nickel).

Why a landed price

A part that costs 2 EUR plus 6 EUR shipping is not cheaper than one that costs 5 EUR with free shipping. All comparisons here use the total price delivered to Germany. Read more in How prices are compared.
